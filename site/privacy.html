This website

No analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or external fonts. The guide opens in English. A language you explicitly choose is included in the guide’s URL. Built-in translations do not contact a translation service. Choosing More languages opens the public guide text in Google Translate, which follows Google’s own privacy policy. The public website is prepared for Cloudflare Pages hosting. Cloudflare processes connection information such as IP addresses, timestamps, requested URLs and technical security information to deliver and protect the website. We do not use this information for advertising profiles. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy. Websupport remains the domain registrar; it is not the website host.

Contact and email

Questions about privacy, access or deletion: olivecode.ai@gmail.com. If you email us, your address, message and any attachments are processed to answer your enquiry. This inbox uses Gmail, so Google also processes that correspondence under its service terms. Do not email passwords, bot tokens, provider keys or private project files. No mailing list is created from support enquiries.

The app

Cloud-model requests go to your chosen provider under your account and its terms. Local Ollama models run on your computer. Sponsor messages are not selected using your prompts or project files.

Computer control and Telegram

Visible Mac control uses the actual mouse and keyboard in the observed app. Browser control uses a separate browser profile. Screen captures request separate approval. Telegram Remote uses your own bot and a paired private account. Tasks, approval details, code diffs, results and opted-in captures pass through Telegram’s bot service; this is not an end-to-end-encrypted channel. The bot token stays in OliveCode’s local configuration and is not sent to models. Remote mode must be enabled locally, and your computer must stay awake with the listener running.

WhatsApp preview

The optional WhatsApp connection uses your own Meta Business API setup and an explicitly allowed phone number. Tasks, approvals, results and opted-in captures pass through Meta. It requires a separately configured HTTPS webhook and is not a personal-account QR login. Meta eligibility, service terms and charges apply; this preview is not advertised as globally available. Tokens stay in local configuration, outside model prompts.

Sponsor measurement

No sponsor-view reporting is active in this preview. Any future reporting must explain what is sent and provide a user setting before activation.
